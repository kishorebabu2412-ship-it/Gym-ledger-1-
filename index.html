What it does

The register, the bill and the reminder in one place

  • Members and plans

    Monthly, Quarterly, Half-Yearly and Yearly plans are ready on day one. Change the prices or add your own plan.

  • Numbered bills

    Every payment gets an invoice number such as INV-2026-0001, with discount and GST. Open it on a phone or laptop and save it as PDF.

  • WhatsApp reminders

    Sent at 9 AM, three days before, one day before and on the day the plan ends. Each reminder goes out once, so nobody is messaged twice.

  • Renewals without lost days

    If the member is still active, the new plan starts the day after the old one ends. If it has already lapsed, it starts on the day they pay.

  • Pause a member

    Someone travelling for a month can be paused. Paused members get no reminders.

  • Phone and laptop

    Open the link, log in with your password, and add it to the home screen. Staff can use whichever device is nearby.

How it works

From sign-up to renewal

  1. Add the member

    Pick the plan, take the payment. The bill is created and the expiry date is worked out for you.

  2. The ledger watches the dates

    Every morning it checks who is close to expiry, including members you forgot about.

  3. The reminder goes out

    A WhatsApp message reaches the member with the plan name and the last date.

  4. Renew in one tap

    Open the member, tap Renew, take payment. A new numbered bill is made and the dates extend.

Questions

Before you decide

Do my members need to install anything?

No. Only you and your staff use the app. Members just receive WhatsApp messages.

Are the WhatsApp messages really sent?

Yes, through the official WhatsApp Business platform. It needs your gym's WhatsApp Business number and a message template that WhatsApp has approved. We set both up with you. Approval can take a few days.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. The same page works on a phone and a laptop. On a phone, use the browser menu and choose Add to Home Screen.

What if someone renews late?

A member who is still active keeps every paid day: the new plan starts the day after the old one ends. A member whose plan has lapsed starts again on the day they pay.

Can I use my own plans and prices?

Yes. Edit the four starter plans or add new ones, such as a 45-day plan or a couples plan.

Who can see my members' details?

The app is behind a login password that you choose. Each gym gets its own separate database, so your members are never mixed with another gym's.

Can the bill show my GST number or logo?

Bills show discount and GST percentage. If you need your GST number or logo on the invoice, tell us during setup.
